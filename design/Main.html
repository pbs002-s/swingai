Needle breaks, traced to the batch

Every needle installed is logged against its supplier lot. When breaks cluster, StitchSense shows whether the pattern follows the batch, the machine or the operator, so a bad lot is not blamed on a worker.

  • →Break rate per needles issued, per 1,000 running hours and per 10,000 units
  • →No alert until a batch has enough use to judge

Numbers people can trust, checked by people

A camera can be wrong. StitchSense marks what it is unsure about, and a supervisor decides. No pay, bonus or training decision is ever made by the software alone.

How data stays in the factory
No face recognition Workers are known by station assignment or their attendance card, never by their face.
No sound, no live watching Video becomes simple events at the edge. Only a few still images are kept, for review.
Unsure means unsure Low-confidence events are shown as uncertain and never counted as fact.
Workers can dispute A wrong record goes back to review through the supervisor, and the correction is logged.

Installed in your factory, on your server

An edge box on the floor turns video and sensor signals into small events. Your server keeps the database and the dashboards. If the network drops, the edge box keeps 72 hours of events and sends them later.
